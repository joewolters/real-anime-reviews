## ⭐ v1.9.0 — Community Overhaul: gate-0 design study (PROPOSED, awaiting Blake's picks — DURABLE, survives SHIP-OUTPUT rollover)

> **Why here:** the FULL study went to `docs/SHIP-OUTPUT.md` (rolling doc) — banked durably here so no idea is lost. **Status: proposed, NOTHING applied; awaiting Blake's answers to the 7 Qs.** Produced via an 18-agent recon→design→adversarial-review workflow.
