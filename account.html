🌙 Leaving

You can delete your account whenever you want. It happens straight away — there's no waiting period where we quietly keep your things.

What happens when you go

  • Everything you wrote is erased. Your comments, replies, reviews and threads are emptied — the words, the pictures and your name.
  • The empty slots stay where they were, reading [removed by the author]. That's the only way the replies underneath them still make sense.
  • Other members keep their words. Nobody else's post disappears because you left.
  • Your profile, saves, shelves and pictures are deleted outright. So are your votes and appreciates.
  • Letters are closed, not destroyed — half of every conversation belongs to the other person.
  • This cannot be undone, and it can't be reversed by asking.
