v1.8.3 Minor Update
v1.8.3
06/05/2026
  • The site now opens with a welcome "door" on your first visit each session — an atmospheric splash with a custom banner, anime quotes drifting up the sides, and an Enter button to step inside. It won't bug you again while you're browsing.
  • The homepage is reorganized around "Blake's Den" — a proper section header gathering the Top 10 and the latest drop — and sections gently fade in as you scroll.
  • The filter is redesigned: tap-to-toggle chips for genres, tags, and studios, a find-as-you-type box to narrow the options, a "Saved" quick-filter for your watchlist and favorites, a live count of how many anime match, and it remembers your last filter between visits.
  • Search now shows matches as you type — no need to press Enter — and a handful of results sit centered instead of hugging the left edge.
  • New "Continue Where You Left Off" row on the homepage with the last few anime you opened.
  • Opening an anime now shows how many seasons of the franchise Blake actually watched, right under the rating.
  • Card footers got a cleaner look with a gold rating pill, and ratings now line up neatly across each row.
  • Duplicate studio names in the filter are merged into one, and "View All Animes" / the site title now clear any active filters.
v1.7.4→v1.8.2
06/04/2026
  • Reviews can now be written in labelled sections — when they are, jump-to-section pills appear above the review so you can skip straight to Animation, Story, Music, and whatever else you're after.
  • Opening a review's deep-dive (and the character/staff details) blurs the page behind it again — a cleaner, more focused frosted look.
  • Behind the scenes: new admin tools to edit and re-publish existing reviews faster, so the catalog stays accurate and up to date.
  • Scrollbars are now brand-purple instead of the default white — you'll spot them on the detail pop-out and the lists.

  • The anime detail panel now lists a couple more key crew roles.
  • Made the per-season episode headers easier to scan.
  • The site now has its own icon in your browser tab (and when you save it to a phone home screen).
  • You can now add any anime — including individual seasons, movies, and specials — to your watchlist or favorites from its detail view, not just from the main catalog cards.
  • Your account's watchlist and favorites now show cover art for everything you've saved, and a saved season or movie opens its detail view right here on the site.
  • Anime you've saved that have a review now show a green ✓ REVIEWED tag in your watchlist and favorites.
  • Click any episode in a detail view to see its thumbnail and every official place to watch it.

  • The franchise panel (every season, side story, and movie) is now always visible when you open an anime — no extra click to expand it.
  • Clicking a related anime now opens a rich detail view right here on the site — synopsis, characters, staff, trailer, and more like it — instead of sending you elsewhere.
  • Each season, movie, or special can now carry its own dedicated review, shown alongside the overall franchise take.
  • Characters and staff are now clickable for full profiles — bios, credits, and where you've seen them before.

About Me:

Hey people I'm Blake and this has been a pet project of mine for a couple months now and I'm super happy that it's finally out. This orignally started off as a place where I would "just leave my reviews" for my friends/family to look at so they know which anime they should keep an eye on. As you can see it quickly grew out of hand and before I knew it, I was praying for chatGPT to practically vibe code the entire website for me. All that aside I'm SUPER happy with how this turned out. If any information is wrong feel free to reach out to me via Instagram. I plan to continue updating this little site and I hope to see y'all around.
