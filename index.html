v2.1.1 Hotfix
v2.1.1
Hotfix
08/12/2026
  • Your half-written reply stops disappearing. The comment list used to be thrown away and rebuilt every time anyone posted or voted — taking your unfinished reply and your open reply panel with it. It now leaves untouched everything that didn't change, including what you're typing and where your cursor is.
  • The search box has its ✕ back — the header search filters as you type, and clearing it no longer means selecting the text and deleting it.
  • Review notifications now find what they're pointing at even when a rating filter is hiding it.
v2.1.0
Feature Update
08/10/2026
  • You can delete your account — it's in your settings, under Leaving, and it happens straight away. Everything you wrote is erased along with your name and your pictures; the empty slot stays, reading [removed by the author], so the replies underneath it still make sense.
  • Nobody else loses their words when someone leaves. A member deleting their account used to take their whole forum thread with them — and every reply inside it, from everyone. That's fixed. What you shared lives where you posted it.
  • Profiles don't drown in reviews — a pinned review leads the page and the rest live behind one "all reviews" button. Copy the address bar while that list is open and the link lands right there.
  • iPhone and Safari, properly — on phones under ~390px wide (the iPhone SE included) the account button sat off the edge of the screen, so signing in was impossible. Tapping a filter box also zoomed the whole page, and the search text rendered clipped mid-word. All fixed and measured on three iPhone sizes.
  • A profile bug with no workaround — members from before profiles existed could never save theirs; every attempt failed silently. Their next save fixes itself, with nothing for them to do.
  • Quote bubbles no longer crowd phones, the catch-up strip stopped overlapping the update log, the last plain dropdowns became branded ones, and the header search bar is proportional again at every width.

Hotfix

  • Four reviews restored — Rascal Does Not Dream of Bunny Girl Senpai, An Archdemon's Dilemma, Attack on Titan and Demon Slayer were showing older, shorter text than Blake wrote. The full versions are back.

ABOUT ME 自己紹介

Hey people! I'm Blake and I've been working on this little pet project of mine for a while now. This originally started as a place where I would leave my personal reviews of the anime I've watched so my close friends and family could know what to look for next. It's grown to be a little bigger than that now... and it's something I could have never dreamed of. I was only able to do this with immense help from Claude and I'm super happy with how it turned out. I plan to keep updating this site and I hope to see y'all around!
